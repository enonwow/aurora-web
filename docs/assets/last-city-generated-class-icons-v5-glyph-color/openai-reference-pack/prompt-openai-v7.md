# OpenAI Prompt: Last City Class Icons v7

Use the attached images as references only. The newest reference image is the current best draft: it has the right dark painted MMO icon mood and good class-color families. Improve it, do not copy it exactly.

Primary goal:
Create a new 5 columns x 3 rows sprite sheet of 15 original fantasy RPG class icons for a Neverwinter Nights / Last City UI. The final image must be a clean sprite sheet that can be cropped into 15 separate 32x32 class icons.

Style target:
- Compact hand-painted MMO skill icons.
- Dark smoky square tile background.
- High contrast, bright rim light, strong black silhouette.
- Saturated class-color glow.
- Gritty fantasy UI mood, close to Last City/GW2-like ability icon color language.
- More "icon glyph" than "illustration".
- Original artwork only. Do not copy or recreate Guild Wars 2 icons or any existing game assets.

Hard layout requirements:
- Exact 5 columns x 3 rows.
- 15 equal square tiles.
- Thin, consistent dark gutters between tiles.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.
- No labels inside or outside tiles.
- Each icon must be centered.
- Each symbol should fill 70-82% of its tile.
- Leave 8-12% dark padding around the symbol so cropping will not cut highlights.
- Do not let glows, weapons, sparks, hands, masks, or rings touch the tile edges.
- Keep every tile visually separate. No element may cross into another tile.

32x32 readability requirements:
- Design for 32x32 first, not for large illustration.
- Use one primary symbol per tile.
- Strong silhouette must still be readable if the sheet is downscaled to 160x96 pixels.
- Avoid tiny scratches, tiny runes, small bubbles, thin ornaments, noisy sparks, and detailed faces.
- Prefer bold shapes, large value blocks, and clean outlines.
- Metallic/glass texture is allowed, but it must not obscure the symbol.

Common mistakes to avoid:
- Do not make character portraits.
- Do not make full-body characters.
- Do not make cinematic scenes.
- Do not make the icons look like card art.
- Do not overfill the frame with glow.
- Do not add UI labels or written class names.
- Do not make all classes use the same circular badge shape.
- Do not make the warrior, shieldmaster, and knight too similar.
- Do not make scientist and alchemist too similar.
- Do not make charlatan look like assassin.

Color families:
- Warrior family: orange, ember, iron, warm steel highlights.
- Tracker family: toxic emerald green, dark natural shadow, sharp green rim light.
- Scientist family: cold cyan, blue steel, glass highlights.
- Trickster family: violet, magenta, deep purple shadow.

Tile order, left to right, top to bottom:

1. Warrior: crossed short sword over round shield. Orange sparks. The sword silhouette should dominate; shield is secondary.
2. Shieldmaster: massive tower shield front view. Thick dark outline, orange edge glow. No sword.
3. Berserker: brutal double axe head with cracked rage mark. Orange-red flame halo. No person, no face.
4. Knight: closed knight helmet with a small lance tip behind it. Orange steel glint. No body.
5. Tracker: compass needle inside a trap ring with a subtle paw notch. Emerald glow. Make it distinct from Tamer.
6. Archer: curved bow and single arrow. Simple diagonal green composition. No quiver, no person.
7. Hunter: claw mark crossed with arrowhead. Toxic green glow. Make the claw mark the dominant shape.
8. Tamer: beast paw print inside a collar ring with a small tag/whistle. Emerald glow. Make it warmer and animal-focused.
9. Scientist: glass vial crossed with atom orbit. Cyan sparkle. Make it precise and analytical.
10. Alchemist: round flask with reagent swirl. Icy blue glow. Make it more liquid/magical than Scientist.
11. Medic: bold medical cross over open palm. Cyan healing ring. Cross must be readable at 32x32.
12. Engineer: large cog wheel with compact crossbow silhouette inside. Blue steel. Gear and crossbow must both be readable at 32x32; no gun.
13. Charlatan: theatrical half-mask with one card corner and a sand flash. Violet glow. Mask should be symbolic, not a detailed face portrait.
14. Boxer: wrapped fist impact glyph. Violet shock ring. No full arm or body.
15. Assassin: dagger under hooded eye crescent. Violet shadow. No full character portrait.

Quality direction:
- Keep the strong polish and color intensity of the current best draft.
- Improve crop safety, symbol clarity, and 32x32 readability.
- Reduce noisy texture and unnecessary fine detail.
- Make the result feel like a coherent class icon set that belongs beside real Last City ability icons.
- The final output should look good as a large sheet, but it must still work as 15 tiny 32x32 icons.
